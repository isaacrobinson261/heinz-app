Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-13

HEINZ is a personal automation assistant built and used solely by its developer, Isaac Robinson. It is not distributed to the public and has no other users.

HEINZ connects to Google services (Calendar, Tasks, Gmail) using OAuth on behalf of the developer only, to read and manage the developer's own calendar, tasks, and email. This data is used exclusively to provide the developer's personal assistant functionality and is never sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.

All data accessed through these integrations is processed locally and is not stored on any external server other than the developer's own infrastructure.

Questions can be directed to the developer via GitHub: github.com/isaacrobinson261.
